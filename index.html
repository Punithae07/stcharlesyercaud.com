Our Legacy of Excellence

Welcome to St. Charles
school - CBSE Yercaud

St. Charles School attracted more and more students because of its good standard and discipline. Additional classes were a necessity. The foundation stone for a new school building was laid by Rev. Fr. Irudaya Selvam the Parish Priest on August 22, 2002 on the Feast of Queenship of our Mother Mary. Rev. Sr. Saroj the Provincial Superior was present on this occasion. The school building was completed and blessed on June 14, 2003. Rocca Borromeo community had the joy and privilege of celebrating the Golden Jubilee of the Religious profession of Sr. Renee and Sr. Peter in1995 and of Sr. Assunta in 2009, Silver Jubilee of Sr. Michael and Sr. Annie Joseph and a rare occasion of celebrating the Diamond Jubilee of Sr. Peter in 2005.
